SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK

Enter one of Africa’s greatest wildlife landscapes, where endless golden plains, dramatic kopjes, vast herds, and some of the world’s most extraordinary predator-prey encounters unfold beneath an open Tanzanian sky.

Explore Destination
The Sanctuary Profile

The Greatest Wildlife Movement

Serengeti National Park protects one of the most celebrated wildlife ecosystems on Earth. Its vast grasslands, woodlands, rivers, kopjes, and seasonal plains support enormous populations of wildebeest, zebra, gazelle, elephants, giraffes, lions, cheetahs, leopards, hyenas, and countless other species. The park is also central to the annual Great Migration.

Endless Plains Spectacles

What You Will Witness

MIGRATION
The Great Terrestrial Run

THE WILDEBEEST MARATHON

Witness the raw chaos of over two million wildebeests, zebras, and gazelles dynamic tracking across the plains. Depending on the season, you will see massive columns plunging into the crocodile-infested waters of the Mara or Grumeti rivers.

PREDATOR
The Big Cat Capital

KOPJE-DWELLING PREDATORS

Scan ancient granite rock outcrops (kopjes) rising like islands from the grass plains. You will observe pride of lions using them as elevated hunting platforms, leopards lounging in nearby acacia forks, and cheetahs tracking prey across open horizons.

MEGAFAUNA
The Unfenced Horizon

SAVANNA GIANTS MARATHON

Experience the vastness of an entirely unfenced wilderness ecosystem. You will watch immense family herds of African elephants migrating across the valleys, tower blocks of giraffes browsing thorn trees, and massive defense walls of Cape buffalos.

The Serengeti Plains Encounters

AQUATIC GIANTS
Submerged River Pods

SERONERA HIPPO POOLS

Watch massive pods of territorial hippos huddle, grunt, and splash inside deep river bends, creating a lively spectacles as they jostle for space beneath the midday sun.

REPTILES
Prehistoric River Predators

RETIMA NILE CROCODILES

Observe giant, armor-plated Nile crocodiles sunbathing along muddy banks or floating like logs near major waterways, waiting patiently for thirsty animals to approach the edge.

SCAVENGERS
Twilight Twilight Prowlers

HYENAS & SIDE-STRIPED JACKALS

Track packs of clever spotted hyenas and side-striped jackals slinking through low brush as dusk settles, playing their essential cleanup roles across the open ecosystem.
