Data Integrity & Your Control

Your personal parameters are guarded metrics. We establish clean operational safeguards to ensure your safari planning remains private, safe, and tailored strictly to your individual consent metrics.

Arena Africa Safaris takes your privacy with maximum seriousness. This policy frames the parameters governing the personal information you intentionally provide—such as names, addresses, email channels, and telephone metrics.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data structures help us deliver an exceptional, curated travel experience. We utilize this context to fulfill reservation pathways, respond to itinerary designs, and send curated offers or customized safari recommendations based on your explicit consent preferences.

You retain an absolute, ongoing right to opt out of our communications at any given moment. Every correspondence or analytical newsletter we dispatch contains a functional, instant unsubscribe element for seamless control.

4. Internet-Collected Information

When navigating our web systems, our architectures automatically log aggregate, completely anonymous metrics, including:

▪ Search configurations and reference phrases utilized to discover our platform.

▪ Inbound URL tracking metrics (the specific website location you arrived from).

▪ Duration of your visit and pages viewed.

▪ Browser type and device information.

This data is aggregated and used solely for analytical purposes to improve our online experience and better serve our guests. No personally identifiable information is linked to this data.
