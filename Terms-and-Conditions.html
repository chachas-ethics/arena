Trip Operations & Local Variables

Please note that operational conditions in Tanzania and East Africa differ fundamentally from developed countries. We remain exposed to unannounced changes in government-regulated taxes and park structures.

1. Operational Parameters & Alterations

Safari services agreed upon will be fully provided, except during unforeseen force majeure events (e.g., road closures, severe weather, security considerations). The itinerary can be changed at company discretion; clients will be kept informed and equivalent alternatives provided. Arena Africa Safaris holds the statutory right to pass any unannounced government-regulated park fees or tax increases directly onto customers.

2. Booking Procedures & Contractual Nature

Arena Africa Safaris specializes exclusively in customized, tailor-made holiday architectures to fulfill personal needs, rather than pre-packaged holiday deals. A legally binding contract establishes itself only upon receipt of your initial deposit or complete payment.

3. Financial Framework & Payment Terms

Confirmed bookings generate a descriptive commercial invoice. Required milestones include:

  • 45% Deposit: Required immediately to confirm reservation spaces.
  • 100% Upfront: Required instantly for any flight segments.
  • 65 Days Prior: Balance payment deadline.

*Bookings made within 65 days of departure demand instant, complete prepayment at the moment of reservation. All payments must be cleared via bank wire/transfer.

After your booking has been confirmed, should you wish to make any changes to your itinerary or wish an earlier departure date, we will make every effort to accommodate your requests based on availability. However, the extra costs involved to make changes are €150.

5. Website Pricing & Quotations

All prices published on arenaafricasafaris.com are per person, based on two persons (unless otherwise stated), and quoted in US dollars (US$). Costs of obtaining passports or visas, border taxes (if applicable), tips for guides, drivers, and hotel staff, insurance, additional excursions, or personal expenses are not included in the itinerary cost. Discount rates for children or peak rates during high/festive seasons are not included.

All prices on the website should be used as a guideline only. Arena Africa Safaris cannot be held responsible for any changes or pricing errors. Arena Africa Safaris will specify all costs in their invoices. The quotations are valid for two weeks.

6. Travel Documents

You receive all travel documents (vouchers, e-tickets, luggage labels, and flyers) two weeks before departure. Please check your confirmation invoice and travel documents immediately upon receipt. Contact us if there are errors. We are not liable if notified after 14 days of issuance.

7. Insurances

It is a condition of booking that the sole responsibility lies with the guest to ensure that they carry the correct comprehensive travel and medical insurance to cover themselves, as well as any dependents or traveling companions, for the duration of their trip to Tanzania/Africa. This insurance should include medical expenses and repatriation expenses.

Optional insurances are cancellation or curtailment of the safari, emergency evacuation expenses (flying doctors), and damage/theft/loss of personal baggage, money, and goods. Arena Africa Safaris may cancel your booking if such insurance is not obtained.

8. Passports, Visas & Name Checks

It is upon the guest to ensure that passports and visas are valid for the countries visited. Most nationalities can get a tourist visa on arrival in Tanzania at the border/airport ($50 per person, US citizens $100 per person). This visa is valid for a period of three months. You can also get your visa in advance at the embassy in your home country. Check the visa rules before you go. Passports should have at least six months of validity after the final day of travel. Arena Africa Safaris cannot be held liable for any visas not held by the guests, nor the cost of visas.

Please check the names on the invoice carefully when you receive our invoice. In case we need to change a name three or more days after you receive our invoice, we are obligated to charge a fee of €125.

9. Medical Conditions and Disabilities

Please inform us of any medical condition or disability that may affect your holiday before confirming your booking so we can assess suitability. We reserve the right to decline bookings or cancel them if we cannot accommodate specific needs.

We reserve the right to cancel your holiday before full payment has been received. In such cases, we'll refund all monies or offer an alternative. We are not liable for cancellations caused by force majeure (war, natural disasters, pandemics, etc.).

If a major change occurs (e.g., flight delay over 12 hours), we'll offer alternatives or a refund. No compensation is payable for changes due to force majeure.
