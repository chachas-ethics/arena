Baobab Migration Luxury Camp

Mbalibali, Serengeti National Park, Tanzania

(5.0 Wilderness Rating)

Our camp offers a remarkable safari experience where you can immerse yourself in nature and see amazing wildlife. The stunning scenery will captivate you completely, as Baobab Migration Luxury Camp is surrounded by ancient acacia trees and looks directly over the sweeping plains.

Our large, custom-engineered tents provide a truly luxurious safari experience. Imagine waking up to a breathtaking African sunrise enjoyed directly from your private veranda.

The camp’s cuisine serves as a definitive culinary highlight. Our professional chefs create a memorable experience by beautifully combining refined international flavors with organic local ingredients. Our dining pavilion will satisfy your palate, whether you’re sharing a hearty breakfast before a thrilling early morning excursion or enjoying a romantic candlelit dinner underneath the bright African stars.

Expect an unforgettable journey. Our expert guides will accompany you on thrilling Serengeti expeditions to track and view the world-renowned Great Migration. Witness millions of wildebeest and other fascinating animals roaming across the endless plains in search of fresh grazing—a sight engineered to amaze and leave lasting lifetime memories.
