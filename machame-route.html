Expedition Brief

The
Machame Route

Including acclimatization hikes and distinctive key highlights such as the impressive Lava Tower, scenic Shira Plateau and challenging Barranco Wall, the Machame Route is one of the most popular and rewarding routes to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Estimated Price
$2,499 USD / PERSON*
01. ENTRY

Machame Gate

Begin the expedition from the southern entrance of Kilimanjaro National Park.

02. SHIRA

Shira Cave Camp

Ascend through changing terrain toward the spectacular Shira Plateau.

03. HIGH ALTITUDE

Lava Tower

Reach 4,600 m before descending toward Barranco Camp, using the climb-high-sleep-low principle for acclimatization.

04. BARRANCO

Barranco Wall

One of the route's defining challenges before continuing toward Karanga Camp.

05. BASE CAMP

Barafu Camp

High-altitude summit camp where you prepare for the midnight summit attempt.

06. SUMMIT

Stella Point → Uhuru Peak

5,756 m → 5,895 m. The final ascent to the Roof of Africa.

Expedition Log

Day-to-Day Itinerary

These itineraries are a starting point to help you plan your Kilimanjaro adventure. Arena Africa Safaris specializes in creating tailor-made tours and personalized adventures fully customized around your personal preferences.

DAY 00

Arrival

Arrival & Pre-Climb Briefing

  • 01 Upon landing at Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO), our professional team will transfer you to your accommodation in Arusha, the gateway to Mount Kilimanjaro.
  • 02 Meet your certified Kilimanjaro guide for a detailed pre-climb briefing and comprehensive gear check.
  • 03 Enjoy a nourishing meal and get some rest before beginning the trek.
DAY 01

Machame Camp

Machame Gate → Machame Camp

After breakfast, your mountain crew will drive you to Machame Gate at 1,800 m. After registering and obtaining permits, begin your trek toward Machame Camp at 2,835 m.

The route passes through lush rainforest, where rain is possible and wildlife may be encountered. Dinner and an overnight stay await at Machame Camp.

Altitude

1,800 → 2,835 M

Distance

11 KM

Duration

5–6 HRS

DAY 02

Shira

Machame Camp → Shira Cave Camp

Start with breakfast at Machame Camp before gradually ascending toward Shira Cave Camp at 3,750 m.

As the rainforest disappears, the spectacular Shira Plateau opens up, providing the first panoramic views of the mountain.

Altitude

2,835 → 3,750 M

Distance

5 KM

Duration

4–5 HRS

Acclimatization Protocol

Hike Toward Lava Tower Camp

In the afternoon, take a short acclimatization hike toward Lava Tower Camp, gaining more than 300 metres before returning to Shira Cave Camp for dinner and overnight rest.

ALTITUDE

3,750 → 4,120 → 3,750 M

DISTANCE

2 KM

DURATION

1–2 HRS

DAY 03

Barranco

Lava Tower → Barranco Camp

Continue from Shira Cave Camp toward Lava Tower at 4,600 m, one of the defining highlights of the Machame Route.

Spend 1–2 hours at Lava Tower to acclimatize while enjoying lunch before descending toward Barranco Camp at 3,900 m.

Altitude

3,750 → 4,600 → 3,900 M

Distance

10 KM

Duration

6–8 HRS

DAY 04

Karanga

Barranco Wall → Karanga Camp

After an early breakfast, begin the climb of the famous Barranco Wall at 4,202 m.

After reaching the top, take photographs with the majestic Kibo volcano behind you before descending toward Karanga Camp at 3,995 m for lunch.

Altitude

3,900 → 4,202 → 3,995 M

Distance

6 KM

Duration

4–5 HRS

Acclimatization Protocol

Hike Toward Barafu Camp

Take a short acclimatization hike toward Barafu Camp at 4,270 m before descending back to Karanga Camp for dinner and overnight rest.

ALTITUDE

3,995 → 4,270 → 3,995 M

DISTANCE

2.5 KM

DURATION

1–2 HRS

DAY 05

Barafu

Karanga Camp → Barafu Camp

After breakfast, continue from Karanga Camp at 3,995 m toward Barafu Summit Camp at 4,673 m.

Known for chilly winds and freezing temperatures, Barafu marks the high-altitude staging point before the summit attempt.

Altitude

3,995 → 4,673 M

Distance

4 KM

Duration

4–5 HRS

Acclimatization Protocol

Hike Toward Kosovo Camp

Take another short acclimatization hike toward Kosovo Summit Camp at 4,800 m before returning to Barafu Camp. Spend the evening resting in preparation for the summit attempt at night.

ALTITUDE

4,673 → 4,800 → 4,673 M

DISTANCE

2 KM

DURATION

1–2 HRS

Summit Operation

Uhuru Peak

5,895 METRES. ROOF OF AFRICA

Conquering Heights

Depart from Barafu Camp at approximately midnight and begin the summit push toward Uhuru Peak, passing Stella Point at 5,756 m along the way.

The final stretch can be demanding due to the extreme altitude, but the reward is reaching the highest point in Africa and witnessing sunrise from the summit.

Altitude

4,673 → 5,756 → 5,895 M

Distance

5 KM

Duration

6–7 HRS

5,895 M
DAY 05. DESCENT

Mweka

Uhuru Peak → Barafu → Mweka Camp

After celebrating your achievement and taking a well-deserved rest, begin descending toward Barafu Camp before continuing to Mweka Camp at 3,100 m.

The descent is faster but can be challenging because of the steep trail, particularly after the long morning spent trekking toward the summit.

Altitude

5,895 → 4,673 → 3,100 M

Distance

10 KM

Duration

6–7 HRS

* 2 HOURS OF REST AT BARAFU CAMP EXCLUDED.

DAY 06

Completion

Mweka Camp → Mweka Gate

Upon reaching Mweka Gate at 1,640 m, celebrate your incredible achievement of reaching Mount Kilimanjaro's summit and receive your official certificates.

Transfer back to your hotel in Arusha for a relaxing shower, dinner and overnight stay.

Altitude

3,100 → 1,640 M

Distance

10 KM

Duration

3–4 HRS
