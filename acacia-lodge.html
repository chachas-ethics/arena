The Acacia Migration Camp

Kogatende & Ndutu, Serengeti, Tanzania

The Acacia Migration Camp has 19 Safari tents, including four Family Tents and one Honeymoon Tent. From June to October, the camp is in the Kogatende area, with additional tents erected at Ndutu from November to March. In keeping with its name, the camp optimizes your immersion into the rhythms and drama of the Great Migration.

At Acacia Migration Camp, guests are accommodated in spacious and airy Safari Tents. Between activities, pre-dinner drinks are served at the Bar before fabulous meals are enjoyed either outdoors or in the large Dining Tent. You’ll have much to share with others about the incredible sightings of the migration seen throughout the day.

Migration-Based Tracking Location:

June – October: Kogatende Area
November – March: Ndutu Region
