SEYCHELLES ISLANDS

Escape to the pristine shores of the Seychelles, where granite boulders, turquoise waters, lush rainforested hills, and powdery white sands create an idyllic paradise far removed from the everyday world.

The Sanctuary Profile

The Archipelago

The Seychelles is a pristine island experience in the Indian Ocean centered around granite islands and coral atolls wrapped in lush, tropical landscapes. The experience is suited to island-hopping, snorkeling along vibrant reefs, nature photography, coastal hiking, and relaxing on secluded white-sand shores.

Archipelago Marvels

What You Will Witness

COASTLINE
Gondwana Relics

BOULDER-LINED SHORES

Witness breathtaking coastal landscapes dominated by massive, smooth, jet-black and pinkish granite boulders. Sculpted by millions of years of wind and waves, these ancient rock formations frame pristine white sands like La Digue’s famous Anse Source d’Argent.

AQUATIC
Oceanic Sanctuaries

VIBRANT MARINE REEFS

Peer through exceptionally clear turquoise waters to look down at protected coral tables. You will watch schools of multicolored parrotfish, rays, and friendly hawksbill sea turtles navigating safely through zero-drift shallow marine national parks.

SANCTUARY
Protected Island Rookeries

ENDEMIC BIRD COLONIES

Visit strict nature islands like Cousin or Aride to see thousands of nesting seabirds completely unafraid of humans. You will stand inches away from breeding fairy terns balancing eggs on bare tree limbs, white-tailed tropicbirds, and giant magpie robins.

The Seychelles Eco-Encounters

MEGAFAUNA
Ancient Free-Roaming Giants

CURIEUSE GIANT TORTOISES

Walk among hundreds of majestic Aldabra giant tortoises roaming freely across this strict bio-reserve, observing their nesting grounds alongside red mangrove swamp boardwalks.

UNESCO PALMS
Prehistoric Primeval Jungles

VALLÉE DE MAI COCO DE MER

Step under the dense canopy of Praslin’s ancient, untouched forest to witness the legendary Coco de Mer palms, producing the largest and heaviest botanical seeds known to earth.

CORAL WILDERNESS
Primal Oceanic Ecosystems

ALDABRA REEF SANCTUARIES

Explore the waters of the world's second-largest raised coral atoll, where highly preserved marine sanctuaries shield massive populations of manta rays, green turtles, and reef sharks.
