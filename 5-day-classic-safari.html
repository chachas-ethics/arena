TANZANIA CLASSIC SAFARI

5-Day Classic

Embark on a definitive journey across northern Tanzania's wildlife strongholds. This premium expedition delivers total immersion, transitioning flawlessly from the giant baobabs of Tarangire to the legendary endless savannah plains of the Serengeti, culminating in the rich caldera basin of the Ngorongoro Crater.

EXPEDITION ROUTE KIA • Arusha • Tarangire • Serengeti National Park • Ngorongoro Crater • Arusha
LOGISTICAL HIGHLIGHT Includes direct airport meet-and-greet reception with private transfers to Arusha outpost
Enquire About This Tour
DAY 1 • KILIMANJARO INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT TO ARUSHA

Your Tanzanian Adventure Begins

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your safari representative and professional driver-guide. From the airport, you will travel to Arusha, the gateway to Tanzania's Northern Safari Circuit. The drive offers your first introduction to Tanzania, with views of local communities, farms and the landscapes surrounding Mount Meru.

After arriving at the lodge, check in and take time to rest after your journey. If your arrival time allows, you may enjoy a relaxed introduction to Arusha or simply prepare for the safari beginning the following morning.

INCLUDED MEALS Dinner (Dependent on Arrival)
DAY 2 • ARUSHA TO TARANGIRE NATIONAL PARK

Elephant Country And The Baobab Landscape

After an early breakfast, depart for Tarangire National Park. Your journey takes you through rural Tanzania before entering the wildlife-rich park. Tarangire is famous for its large elephant herds and enormous baobab trees.

During the game drive, explore the Tarangire River and surrounding woodland and grasslands. You may see elephants walking close to the vehicle, giraffes feeding from acacia trees, zebras and wildebeest grazing together, buffalo moving through the vegetation and impalas crossing the plains.

Predators including lions may also be encountered, while the park's diverse birdlife provides constant activity above the grasslands. After lunch, continue your afternoon game drive before leaving the park for your lodge.

OVERNIGHT RETREAT Marela Valley
INCLUDED MEALS Breakfast • Lunch • Dinner
DAY 3 • SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK

The Journey Into The Endless Plains

After breakfast, leave for Serengeti. As you travel deeper into northern Tanzania, the landscape becomes increasingly spectacular. Upon entering Serengeti, begin your game drive through the vast plains.

Look for wildebeest, zebra, gazelles, giraffes and buffalo. Your guide will also search for predators, particularly lions, cheetahs, hyenas and leopards. Depending on the season, you may encounter areas where migration herds are concentrated. Continue towards your accommodation with game viewing along the way.

OVERNIGHT RETREAT Mbuni Tented Camps
INCLUDED MEALS Breakfast • Lunch • Dinner
DAY 4 • FULL-DAY SERENGETI NATIONAL PARK

A Day Of Predators, Herds & Endless Horizons

After breakfast, set out for a full-day game drive with a packed lunch. This is your opportunity to explore Serengeti without rushing. Spend the day following wildlife movements and exploring different habitats.

Your guide may take you towards areas known for lion prides, cheetahs or hyenas, while searching carefully for leopards. Large herbivore herds may include wildebeest, zebra and gazelles, while giraffes, elephants and buffalo can also be encountered.

If travelling during the Great Migration, you may witness huge concentrations of wildebeest and zebra moving through the ecosystem. Return to camp after an unforgettable day.

OVERNIGHT RETREAT Mbuni Tented Camps
INCLUDED MEALS Breakfast • Lunch • Dinner
DAY 5 • SERENGETI TO NGORONGORO CRATER AND ARUSHA

The Final Wildlife Experience

After breakfast, travel towards Ngorongoro. Descend into the crater for an unforgettable wildlife experience. Explore the crater floor's grasslands, wetlands and woodland areas while searching for buffalo, zebra, wildebeest, gazelles, hippos, lions and hyenas.

With luck, you may encounter the endangered black rhinoceros. After the crater experience, begin your journey back to Arusha.
